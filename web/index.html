Tailcat Rust

This is an experimental demo of Tailcat Rust in the browser. Unlike the normal CLI and non-JS library versions, all of its traffic is relayed through DERP servers; browsers can't make tailcat's direct peer-to-peer connections without WebRTC support (issue #4).
